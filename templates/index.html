Intuitive 4-Step Flow

How Share & Reuse Works

Designed to handle time-critical surplus food and category-segregated clothes effortlessly.

1
Donor Lists Items

Donor specifies category (Food or Clothes), quantity, storage conditions, pickup address, and urgent expiry deadlines.

2
Location Matching

Our Haversine distance engine calculates proximity to verified NGOs, orphanages, and textile recyclers in kilometers.

3
Accept & Schedule

Eligible organization accepts the request, assigns volunteer contact details, and schedules estimated arrival.

4
OTP Verified Pickup

Physical handover is secured using a 4-digit verification code. Both parties receive carbon impact and appreciation records.

Urgent Food Rescue

Time-Critical Food Matching

Cooked food is highly perishable. Unlike generic donation platforms, Share & Reuse captures:

  • Preparation Time & Expiry Deadline: Calculates exact hours remaining.
  • Thermal Condition: Hot cases, ambient, refrigerated, or frozen preservation.
  • Automated Urgency Boosting: When remaining time is < 4 hours, listings trigger high-priority alerts to closest shelters.
Smart Clothes Routing

Reusable vs. Recycling Segregation

Clothing donations are classified by condition to ensure zero dumping at charity doors:

  • Reusable & Good Condition: Routed directly to Orphanages, Old-Age Homes & Community NGOs for direct wear.
  • Damaged / Scrap / Fabric Cuts: Automatically directed to Textile Recycling & Upcycling units for industrial reuse, avoiding landfills.
