1. What is "Share & Reuse"? (Elevator Pitch)

Share & Reuse is a location-aware web platform bridging donors having surplus food and reusable/unused clothes with verified organizations (NGOs, orphanages, old-age homes) and textile recycling units.

The Real-World Problem:
  • Commercial banquet halls and mess facilities throw away cooked meals that spoil in 4–6 hours.
  • Donation drives haphazardly dump damaged scrap fabric at orphanages with no way to recycle it.
  • Static NGO lists have no GPS distance ranking or verified handover mechanisms.
Our Solution:
  • Urgent Food Matching: Monitors preparation and expiry timestamps; triggers high-priority alerts when time ≤ 4 hours.
  • Textile Segregation: Channels wearable clothes to orphanages and routes damaged scrap to textile recyclers.
  • Haversine GPS Engine: Calculates road proximity in kilometers and protects handover with a 4-digit OTP PIN.
2. Advantages & Disadvantages (Evaluation Analysis)
Key Advantages
  • Automated Perishability Scoring: Prioritizes food close to expiry to prevent wastage.
  • Circular Economy for Textiles: Prevents synthetic fabrics from accumulating in landfills.
  • Mathematical Geolocation: Uses the spherical Haversine formula instead of flat Euclidean approximations.
  • Anti-Fraud Handover PIN: 4-digit OTP code ensures physical receipt before marking donations completed.
  • Zero API Costs: Free Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap instead of expensive Google Maps billing.
Limitations & Future Enhancements
  • Physical Sensory Inspection: The platform relies on declared food hygiene; driver checks condition at pickup. (Future work: IoT smart gas freshness sensors).
  • Connectivity Dependency: Requires internet for real-time OTP validation. (Future work: Offline SMS/USSD fallback).
